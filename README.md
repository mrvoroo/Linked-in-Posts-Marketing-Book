# Digitally: 30-day LinkedIn content plan

A plan of 100 LinkedIn posts over 30 days for the bundle "The Laid-Off Marketer's 30-Day Job Loss Plan" (book + Google Sheet + Toolkit).

- `index.html`: the interactive site (open in a browser). Filter by day, type and goal; copy any post.
- `posts.json`: all 100 posts with fields (day, slot, type, source, image spec, reviewer scores are in the site).
- `all_posts.txt`: all posts as plain text.
- `images/`: cropped or blurred book and toolkit pages to attach to posts (file name starts with the post number).

Placeholders: `[FREE LINK]` is the free pack link and `[LINK]` is the paid product link. Both go in the first comment, not in the post.
